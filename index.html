About AUREL

A small house with a slow clock.

AUREL began in a single room above a Grasse perfumery, with one rule: never rush a scent. Every batch macerates for eight weeks before it is filtered, poured into weighty glass and capped in solid brass. We make fewer bottles, and we make them properly.

Our five fragrances each follow a moment of light, from the first gold of dawn to amber candlelight, so you can choose a scent the way you would choose an hour.
