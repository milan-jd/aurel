# AUREL: concept build

A concept build: a premium multi-page D2C fragrance store for **AUREL**, a maison de parfum. It is a portfolio piece by **Milan John Davis**.

**Live site:** https://milan-jd.github.io/aurel/

## Pages

- **Home**: brand story, the collection, a scroll-driven ritual gallery, reviews, Discovery Set, FAQ and newsletter.
- **Shop**: all scents with family filters and sorting.
- **Product pages**: photography, description, notes, qualities (longevity, sillage, season), size, free engraving, quantity and add to bag. No. 7 includes a live 3D bottle that shows the engraving as you type.
- **Bag**: quantities, promo code (try `AUREL10`), gift wrap, free sample and totals.
- **Checkout**: details with validation, delivery options, a payment step in demo mode, and an order confirmation.

The bag persists across pages. Checkout is a demo: no card details are collected and no payment is taken.

## A reference build

Client stores build on this with real product photography, Shopify or Stripe checkout, CMS content and richer motion, all scoped to each brief. Product imagery here is AI-generated for the concept.
